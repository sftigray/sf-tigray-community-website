Community Rules & Guidelines

Welcome to the San Francisco Tigray Community! We bring Tigrayans and friends of Tigray together to preserve our heritage, support one another, and build a strong, united community.

All members and website visitors are expected to follow these guidelines.

1. Respect Everyone

Treat everyone with kindness, dignity, and respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

2. Promote Unity and Cooperation

Encourage friendship, understanding, and cooperation. Respect differences of opinion and avoid divisive behavior.

3. Preserve Our Culture and Heritage

Celebrate Tigrayan culture, language, history, traditions, and values. Encourage younger generations to learn about their heritage.

4. Share Accurate Information

Share truthful and relevant news, announcements, and events. Avoid rumors, misleading information, and unverified claims.

5. Respect Privacy

Do not publish personal information, photographs, videos, or contact details of others without appropriate permission.

6. Appropriate Photos and Videos

Submit respectful, relevant content. Obtain appropriate permission before sharing identifiable images, especially of children. Administrators may review submissions.

7. No Hate Speech or Abusive Language

Hate speech, insults, intimidation, and encouragement of violence or hostility are prohibited.

8. Community Events and Participation

Members are encouraged to participate in meetings, cultural celebrations, educational activities, volunteer programs, and community events.

9. No Unauthorized Advertising or Spam

Commercial advertising, repeated promotions, fraudulent fundraising, and unrelated links require administrator approval.

10. Respect Community Leadership and Volunteers

Communicate respectfully with organizers, administrators, volunteers, and members. Constructive feedback is welcome.

11. Responsible Website Use

Do not misuse forms, access restricted information, impersonate others, or interfere with website security.

12. Follow Community Guidelines

Administrators may review, decline, or remove content that violates these rules. Serious or repeated violations may result in restricted access.

Our Community Commitment

Together, we can build a stronger, more united, respectful, and supportive Tigrayan community in San Francisco and the Bay Area.
