A Call for Justice, Civilian Protection, and Lasting Peace in Ethiopia

To the Honorable Members of the United States Senate and House of Representatives

Subject: Urgent Appeal for Congressional Action to Protect Civilians and Support Justice and Lasting Peace Throughout Ethiopia

Dear Members of Congress,

We, members and supporters of the Ethiopian Diaspora, respectfully call upon the United States Congress to take meaningful action to protect innocent civilians throughout Ethiopia, address reports of drone strikes and other attacks affecting civilian communities, support humanitarian assistance, promote accountability for violations of human rights, and encourage justice and lasting peace for all Ethiopians.

Across Ethiopia, reports of armed conflict, drone strikes, civilian casualties, displacement, and human rights abuses have raised serious concerns. Families and communities continue to suffer, underscoring the urgent need for civilian protection, humanitarian assistance, and independent investigations.

Behind every reported attack are human lives, not statistics. There are mothers, fathers, children, brothers, and sisters whose lives have been lost, families separated, and futures shattered. Every civilian deserves safety, dignity, and protection, regardless of ethnicity, religion, or region.

The international community cannot remain silent while civilians throughout Ethiopia face violence, displacement, and humanitarian suffering. Human rights, democracy, justice, and human dignity must be upheld for every Ethiopian, regardless of ethnicity, religion, political affiliation, or geographic location.

The protection of innocent civilians must never depend on nationality, geography, political interests, or economic influence.

Our Appeal to the United States Congress

  1. Demand the protection of innocent civilians

    Call upon all parties to protect civilians throughout Ethiopia, end unlawful attacks, and comply with international humanitarian law.

  2. Support independent investigations and accountability

    Encourage credible, impartial, and transparent investigations into reported drone strikes, civilian casualties, and other alleged violations of international humanitarian and human rights law. Support accountability wherever credible evidence establishes responsibility.

  3. Strengthen humanitarian assistance

    Support continued humanitarian assistance and advocate for safe, sustained, and unhindered humanitarian access so affected families can receive food, medical care, shelter, and other essential services.

  4. Promote diplomatic engagement and lasting peace

    Encourage sustained United States diplomatic engagement aimed at preventing renewed violence, protecting vulnerable communities, supporting peaceful dialogue, and advancing a durable and just peace.

  5. Maintain international attention

    Encourage continued congressional oversight and international attention to the humanitarian and human rights situation throughout Ethiopia, ensuring that the suffering of civilians in every region is not forgotten.

A Call to Humanity

We are not asking for sympathy alone.

We are asking for truth.

We are asking for justice.

We are asking for accountability.

Above all, we are asking for the protection of innocent human lives.

Every mother deserves to raise her children without fear. Every child deserves to grow up in safety. Every family deserves the opportunity to live in peace and dignity.

Those who have lost their lives cannot be brought back. But their suffering must never be forgotten, and their deaths must never be met with silence or indifference.

Silence must never become complicity.

We respectfully appeal to the United States Congress to demonstrate leadership by supporting civilian protection, independent investigations, humanitarian assistance, accountability, and lasting peace for all people of Ethiopia.

All people throughout Ethiopia deserve the same fundamental human rights, dignity, safety, and protection as people everywhere in the world.

THE SUFFERING OF CIVILIANS THROUGHOUT ETHIOPIA MUST NOT BE FORGOTTEN.

JUSTICE FOR ALL VICTIMS THROUGHOUT ETHIOPIA.

PROTECT INNOCENT CIVILIANS.

NEVER FORGET THE LIVES LOST.

Respectfully submitted,

Ethiopian Diaspora

Together with the undersigned supporters of this Congressional Petition.
