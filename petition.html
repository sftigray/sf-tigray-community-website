A Call for Justice, Civilian Protection, and Lasting Peace in Tigray, Ethiopia

To the Honorable Members of the United States Senate and House of Representatives

Subject: Urgent Appeal for Congressional Action to Protect Civilians and Support Justice and Lasting Peace in Tigray

Dear Members of Congress,

We, the undersigned supporters of this petition, respectfully call upon the United States Congress to take meaningful action in response to the continued suffering, humanitarian challenges, and security concerns affecting the people of Tigray, Ethiopia.

The devastating conflict in Tigray has left families grieving, communities traumatized, and countless innocent people struggling to rebuild their lives. Reports of drone strikes and other attacks affecting civilians have raised serious concerns about the safety of vulnerable communities and the urgent need for independent investigations.

Behind every reported attack are human lives, not statistics. There are mothers, fathers, children, brothers, and sisters whose lives have been lost, whose families have been separated, and whose futures have been shattered.

The world cannot continue to speak of human rights, democracy, justice, and human dignity while the suffering of vulnerable communities receives inadequate attention.

The protection of innocent civilians must never depend on nationality, geography, political interests, or economic influence.

Our Appeal to the United States Congress

  1. Demand the protection of innocent civilians

    Call for the protection of civilians in Tigray and urge all parties to comply with international humanitarian law, including the prohibition of unlawful attacks against civilians.

  2. Support independent investigations and accountability

    Encourage credible, impartial, and transparent investigations into reported drone strikes, civilian casualties, and other alleged violations of international humanitarian and human rights law. Support accountability wherever credible evidence establishes responsibility.

  3. Strengthen humanitarian assistance

    Support continued humanitarian assistance and advocate for safe, sustained, and unhindered humanitarian access so affected families can receive food, medical care, shelter, and other essential services.

  4. Promote diplomatic engagement and lasting peace

    Encourage sustained United States diplomatic engagement aimed at preventing renewed violence, protecting vulnerable communities, supporting peaceful dialogue, and advancing a durable and just peace.

  5. Maintain congressional oversight and public attention

    Encourage appropriate congressional hearings, briefings, and continued oversight of the humanitarian and human rights situation in Tigray to ensure that the suffering of civilians does not disappear from international attention.

A Call to Humanity

We are not asking for sympathy alone.

We are asking for truth.

We are asking for justice.

We are asking for accountability.

Above all, we are asking for the protection of innocent human lives.

Every mother deserves to raise her children without fear. Every child deserves to grow up in safety. Every family deserves the opportunity to live in peace and dignity.

Those who have lost their lives cannot be brought back. But their suffering must never be forgotten, and their deaths must never be met with silence or indifference.

Silence must never become complicity.

We respectfully appeal to the United States Congress to demonstrate leadership by supporting civilian protection, independent investigations, humanitarian assistance, accountability, and lasting peace for the people of Tigray.

The people of Tigray deserve the same fundamental human rights, dignity, and protection as people everywhere in the world.

TIGRAY MUST NOT BE FORGOTTEN.

JUSTICE FOR TIGRAY.

PROTECT INNOCENT CIVILIANS.

NEVER FORGET THE LIVES LOST.

Respectfully submitted,

San Francisco Tigray Community

Together with the undersigned supporters of this Congressional Petition.
