Who we are

A place to belong.
A heritage to carry forward.

Our mission is to connect the Tigrayan community through cultural preservation, meaningful relationships, mutual support, and opportunities to participate. We welcome people who want to share in our heritage and help nurture a connected, thriving community.

✧

Honor our heritage

Celebrate Tigrayan culture, language, traditions, and values—and pass them on to future generations.

♡

Strengthen connection

Create welcoming opportunities for people and families to meet, build friendships, and feel at home.

Support one another

Share skills, ideas, and time with a spirit of care, participation, and community responsibility.
